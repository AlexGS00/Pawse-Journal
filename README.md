# Pawse

> *A journal that listens.*

---

Most AI assistants have the memory of a goldfish. You tell them something today, and tomorrow you're starting from scratch — re-explaining yourself, re-establishing context, re-building the conversation.

Pawse is different.

Write in your journal, and your words become the AI's long-term memory. Ask it about a feeling you described three weeks ago. It was there. Ask it to connect a pattern you noticed last month to something you're going through now. It can. Your journal entries aren't just notes — they're the foundation of every conversation you have.

---

## The idea

Most journaling apps are passive. You write, it stores. That's it.

Pawse treats your journal as a living document — a personal knowledge base that an AI can actually read, reason about, and reference. When you open a chat, the AI isn't starting cold. It's read your entries. It knows what matters to you, in your words, from your life.

The name is a play on *pause to reflect*. The cat has nothing to do with it. (The cat has everything to do with it.)

---

## What it does

- **Write entries** — a clean, distraction-free space to put your thoughts down.
- **Chat with context** — start a conversation tied to a specific entry, or just open a standalone chat. Either way, the AI draws from your entire journal when it answers.
- **Your words, your memory** — nothing is summarized away or lost. The AI retrieves the actual passages most relevant to what you're asking about.
- **Conversations that persist** — come back to a chat days later and pick up where you left off. Rename it. Delete it. It's yours.

---

## Who made this

A high school student who wanted a journaling app that actually *got it* — one that understood that the point of writing things down is so you don't have to hold them in your head forever.

Built as a portfolio project. Kept small on purpose. No features for the sake of features.

---

## A note on aesthetic

Minimal. Warm. A little literary. The kind of app that feels like a quiet corner, not a dashboard.

No noise. No clutter. Just you and your thoughts — with a very well-read AI in the room.
